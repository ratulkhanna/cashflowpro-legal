Privacy Policy

CashFlow Pro — by BRIGHTWIZZ

Last updated: May 2026

BRIGHTWIZZ ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CashFlow Pro mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address: Collected at sign-up via Google Sign-In for authentication and account identification. We do not collect your phone number, government ID, or any information beyond what Google provides during sign-in.
  • Transaction data: Cash in and cash out entries you create, including amounts, remarks, dates, categories, payment modes, and any attached images or PDF files you choose to upload.
  • Business and book names: The names you give to your businesses and cash books within the App.
  • Team member email addresses: Email addresses of people you invite to collaborate on a book. These are entered by you and used solely to send the invitation.
  • Device data: We do not collect device identifiers, location data, contacts, advertising IDs, or any data beyond what you directly enter into the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you securely using Google Sign-In.
  • To store and sync your financial records across your devices.
  • To enable collaboration features when you invite team members to a book.
  • To send you in-app notifications about invitations (when the App is open).
  • To provide activity logs of changes made to shared books.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the App.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Server location: Your data is stored on servers operated by Supabase Inc., located in Singapore (ap-southeast-1 region).
  • Encryption in transit: All communication between the App and our servers uses TLS (HTTPS) encryption.
  • Encryption at rest: Data stored on Supabase servers is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • On-device storage: A local copy of your data is stored in a SQLite database on your device, protected by Android's application security sandbox.
  • Access control: Row-level security policies ensure that users can only access data they own or have been explicitly invited to.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is shared only in these limited cases:

  • Team members you invite: If you invite another user to a book, they will be able to see the transactions in that book according to the role and permissions you assign.
  • Service providers: We use Supabase Inc. (database and authentication infrastructure) and Google LLC (authentication via Google Sign-In). These providers process your data only to the extent necessary to provide their services.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by applicable law, court order, or government authority.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data — including businesses, books, transactions, and activity logs — is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. Backups are purged on a rolling basis within the same period.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: All your data is visible within the App at any time.
  • Export your data: Use the Export feature within any book to download your transactions as CSV or PDF.
  • Delete your account and data: Go to Settings → Delete Account within the App. This permanently deletes all your data from our servers. This action cannot be undone.
  • Correct your data: Edit or delete any transaction directly within the App.

7. Children's Privacy

CashFlow Pro is intended for use by business owners and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you through the App. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
